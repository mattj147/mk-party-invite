Rounds of Kombat The Order of Battle

🕹️
Round 1 — 12:00 PM
Arcade & Console Kombat
The tournament begins! Doors open at noon. Step into the arena and prove your worth across generations of Mortal Kombat — from the classic arcade trilogy that started it all, to MK11 and Mortal Kombat 1 on console. Test your might on the sticks, or grab a controller and show these Earthrealm warriors what you're made of. All skill levels welcome — there are no mercy rules here.
🎬
Round 2 — 1:00 PM Sharp
Mortal Kombat (2021) Screening
"Get over here!" The screen lights up at 1:00 PM sharp with the 2021 Mortal Kombat film. Watch Cole Young discover his arcana, Kano deliver the one-liners, and Sub-Zero and Scorpion settle their centuries-old blood feud in one of the most brutal fight sequences ever filmed. Jax, Sonya, Kung Lao, Liu Kang — the whole roster shows up. Snacks and refreshments provided, because even warriors need to refuel between rounds.
🎟️
Round 3 — 4:15 PM
Mortal Kombat 2 — In Theatres
We take the fight to the big screen. Mortal Kombat 2 is now in theatres and we are going as a squad. The Outworld invasion continues — will Liu Kang finally face Shang Tsung in the true tournament? Will Johnny Cage finally make his entrance? Will Kitana betray Outworld? Only one way to find out. Showtime: 4:15 PM. Do not be late — Shang Tsung does not wait.
🍻
Final Round — Evening
Flawless Victory & Fight Night
The warriors return from the theatre for the final round. Drinks flow, the games are back on, and we fire up Netflix for a real-world fight card. The main event: Ronda Rousey vs. Gina Carano — two of the most dangerous women on the planet throwing down. Whether you're still gaming, watching the fights, or just soaking it all in — the Kombat doesn't end until you say "Fatality."
